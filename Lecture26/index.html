Gulab jamun

Ingredients:

  • Shuger
  • Bread
  • Oil

Recipe:

  1. Prepare dough: Mix khoya, maida, and a pinch of baking soda. Add milk to make a soft dough and roll into small balls.
  2. Fry: Deep fry the balls on low flame until golden brown.
  3. Make syrup: Boil sugar and water to make light sugar syrup; add cardamom and rose water.
  4. oak & serve: Put fried jamuns into warm syrup and soak for 1–2 hours. Serve.

Modak

Ingredients:

  • Rice
  • Water
  • Ghee
  • Salt

Recipe:

  1. Prepare stuffing: Cook coconut and jaggery together; add cardamom and keep aside.
  2. Make dough: Boil water with ghee and salt, add rice flour, mix well, and knead into soft dough.
  3. Shape modak: Flatten dough, add stuffing, and shape into modaks.
  4. Steam: Steam modaks for 10–12 minutes and serve hot.
